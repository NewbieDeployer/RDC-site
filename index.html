How To Buy RDC?

  • Step 1: Set Up Your Wallet
    • Download MetaMask (or any wallet that supports the Base chain).
    • Make sure your wallet is connected to the Base chain network.
    • Deposit ETH or other supported tokens into your wallet.
  • Step 2: Visit a DEX
    • Go to Uniswap or a compatible DEX like BaseSwap.
    • Ensure the DEX is set to the Base chain network.
  • Step 3: Select RDC Token
    • Search for RDC by its contract address (add it manually if not listed).
    • Select RDC to add it to your trade.
  • Step 4: Buy RDC
    • Enter the amount of ETH you want to spend.
    • Confirm the transaction in MetaMask after reviewing the details.
    • Click Swap to execute the trade.
  • Step 5: Receive RDC
    • Once the transaction is confirmed, RDC will be in your wallet.
    • You can track the transaction on the Base Explorer.
